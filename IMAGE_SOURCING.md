# Image Sourcing — Direct Imaging

Guidance for adding illustrative images to this repo. Two tracks: real
archival imagery first, AI-generation as a fallback. Every image needs
a caption naming the real mission/instrument depicted — AI-generated
images must say so explicitly and must never name the generating tool.

## Real sources to check first — best option of all four method repos

The actual HR 8799 discovery image (Keck/Gemini adaptive-optics image
showing all four imaged planets) is public and widely reproduced with
credit to Marois et al./Keck Observatory/NRC-Canada. Check:

- Keck Observatory press room — <https://www.keckobservatory.org/press-room/>
- NASA's exoplanet image gallery — <https://exoplanets.nasa.gov/resources/>

This is a **real photograph, not AI** — if used, cite Marois et al.
2008 and Keck/Gemini Observatory directly. No AI-credit caption
needed.

**instrument:** a Keck Observatory or JWST coronagraph instrument
photo (public domain/NASA, or keckobservatory.org press images).

## AI-generation fallback prompts (only if the real HR 8799 image can't be cleared)

- **hero:** "Artist's concept of a young, still-glowing giant
  exoplanet directly imaged as a bright point of light next to a
  coronagraph-blocked star, infrared color palette"
- **3d:** "Close-up render of a hot, young Jupiter-like exoplanet
  glowing orange-red from residual formation heat, deep space
  background"
- **instrument:** "Photorealistic illustration of the Keck
  Observatory dome at night on Mauna Kea, telescope open to starry
  sky"

## Citation rule

- AI-generated: caption states "AI-generated illustration/concept —
  not a real photograph," names the real mission/instrument, never
  names the generating tool.
- Real archival photo (like HR 8799): cite the actual paper and
  observatory directly — no AI-credit line needed.
